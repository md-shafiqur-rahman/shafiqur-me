FAQ

AI Automation Questions

What is AI automation?

AI automation connects the apps your business already uses and adds AI steps where a person used to make a decision. For example, a workflow can capture a new lead, use AI to check if it is a good fit, add it to your CRM and send a personal follow-up, all without anyone touching it.

What can you automate with n8n?

Most repetitive work that moves data between apps, for example:

  • Lead capture, scoring and CRM updates
  • Email and WhatsApp follow-ups
  • Support ticket routing and AI chatbots
  • Weekly reports pulled from several tools
  • Syncing data between forms, spreadsheets, databases and payment tools

Do you use n8n, Make or Zapier?

Mostly n8n, because it handles complex multi-step workflows and can be self-hosted. I also work in Make and Zapier when a business already uses them. I compare the three in n8n vs Make vs Zapier.

How long does it take to build a workflow?

A simple workflow can be live in a few days. Larger systems with AI agents and several connected apps take longer. You get a clear timeline after the free strategy call, before any work starts.

How much does automation cost?

It depends on how many apps and steps are involved, so I give a fixed quote after the free strategy call. My article How much does business automation cost? explains what drives the price.

Can I see workflows you have built?

Yes. My n8n workflows are public on GitHub, and the projects page has screenshots and downloadable workflow files.
