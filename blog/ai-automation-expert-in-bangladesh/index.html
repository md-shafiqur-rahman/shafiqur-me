The Tools Behind the Work

Shafiqur Rahman uses a professional-grade automation stack that most local vendors in Bangladesh are not yet offering:

n8n — A self-hosted, open-source automation platform. Unlike Zapier or Make, n8n runs on your own infrastructure, which means your data stays private, costs don't scale per task, and you get full flexibility to build complex, multi-branch workflows.

OpenAI / GPT-4 — For intelligent decision-making inside workflows. Instead of simple if-then rules, AI-powered automations can read emails, extract key information, classify intent, draft responses, and make smart decisions — just like a human assistant.

AI Agents — Autonomous AI workers that don't just follow rules, but can browse the web, use tools, remember context, and take multi-step actions without human input.

Custom APIs and Webhooks — Connecting any platform — whether it's a local Bangladeshi CRM, a custom-built ERP, or an international SaaS tool — to a centralized automation system.
